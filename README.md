### CHEM-000442: A free phosphatetrazolate [PN₄]⁻ ring
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Release and isolate aromatic [PN₄]⁻ from its metal coordination environment without nitrogen loss or P–N fragmentation. Determine its counterion dependence and decomposition kinetics.
- **Status:** An osmium-bound PN₄ ring was formed in 2025 through phosphorus-mononitride chemistry. Metal-free isolation remains a proposed next target; HCPN₃⁻ and P₂N₃⁻ are different compositions.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Tests whether aromatic stabilization sustains this nitrogen-rich phosphorus heterocycle independently.
- **Key works:** S. Edin et al. (2025), “Unleashing phosphorus mononitride,” [DOI: 10.1038/s41467-025-60669-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-60669-6).
